# User guide

## English
**Install:** open the site on your phone and use "Add to Home screen". After you have opened a page once it still opens without a connection.
**Create an account:** Create account, choose who you are (farmer, buyer, cooperative admin, storage provider, transporter), fill the form, tick consent. Cooperative admins must also set up a two-step code (authenticator app) after signing in; save the recovery codes.
**Farmer:** Home shows the Sell button. Sell asks five things: how many bags, harvest date, quality and place, price (the official reference price is shown beside it, with its source and date), check and publish. Add photos from the listing page. Under Harvest add harvest dates and get a reminder 3 days before. Under Enquiries read, reply, and mark agreed; the buyer's phone is shown after you reply. Under Losses record where and why potatoes were lost; add harvest lots to see that harvested = sold + damaged + spoiled + rejected + unaccounted. Reports show losses by stage; export CSV.
**Working offline:** with no connection you can still list produce, record losses, send enquiries, post a need and report a price. The top bar shows "Offline" and how many items are waiting; they are sent when you are online again (tap Send now). Photos need a connection. Items the server refuses are kept and marked "Needs checking".
**Buyer:** Market: search place or variety, filter by county, minimum bags, quality, sort, tick two or three listings to compare, save suppliers, send an enquiry (bags must not exceed what is free). Post a need and farmers with matching produce are told; each match shows its score out of 100 and why. Enquiries show Sent, Replied, Agreed. FreshRoute never handles payment.
**Storage and transport:** providers list capacity, free space, temperature (self-declared until staff verify), price; transporters list vehicle, capacity, routes, price. Farmers and buyers ask for space or request a delivery; the transporter updates Accepted, Picked up, Delivered by hand.
**Prices:** two tabs. "Official / published source" shows each price with source, link, collection date and licence badge (Synthetic in this demo). "User-submitted" is what users report; it is never part of official figures.
**My data:** download everything as JSON or each part as CSV; request account deletion (7 days to change your mind).

## Kiswahili (DRAFT - needs native-speaker review before any use)
**Kuanza:** fungua tovuti kwenye simu na uchague "Ongeza kwenye skrini ya mwanzo". Kurasa ulizofungua awali hufunguka hata bila mtandao.
**Akaunti:** Fungua akaunti, chagua wewe ni nani, jaza fomu na ukubali. Wasimamizi wa ushirika huweka pia msimbo wa hatua mbili.
**Mkulima:** bonyeza Uza; jibu maswali matano (magunia, tarehe ya mavuno, ubora na mahali, bei, kagua). Bei rasmi ya marejeo huonyeshwa pamoja na chanzo na tarehe. Chini ya Hasara rekodi mahali na sababu ya hasara. Chini ya Maswali jibu wanunuzi; namba yako ya simu huonyeshwa baada ya kujibu.
**Bila mtandao:** unaweza kuweka tangazo, kurekodi hasara na kutuma swali; vitu hutumwa ukiwa mtandaoni tena. Picha zinahitaji mtandao.
**Mnunuzi:** tafuta kwenye Soko, linganisha matangazo hadi matatu, tuma swali, weka hitaji. FreshRoute haishughulikii malipo.
**Bei:** kichupo cha "Chanzo rasmi" na kichupo cha "Zilizotolewa na mtumiaji"; hazichanganywi.
**Data yangu:** pakua data yako au omba akaunti ifutwe (una siku 7 kubadili mawazo).
